Generative AI (GenAI) is a type of artificial intelligence that can create new content such as text, images, audio, video, and code.

It works by learning from huge amounts of data and then generating new content based on the user’s prompt.

GenAI is used in many areas, such as chatbots, coding, data analysis, content creation, translation, and image generation.

In simple words: Traditional AI mainly analyzes or predicts, while Generative AI can create new content.
